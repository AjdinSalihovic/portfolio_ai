Process

How working together goes.

  1. Scope

    A short call or email thread to understand the problem, the data, and what "done" looks like. Free, no commitment.

  2. Proposal

    A written plan: deliverables, timeline, and a fixed or milestone-based price. No surprises later.

  3. Build

    Deployed, documented, and explained. You own the code, and your team knows how to run it.
